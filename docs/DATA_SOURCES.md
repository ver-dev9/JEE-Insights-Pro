## 2026 Paper 1 links
The nine direct PDF URLs in `src/data/papers.ts` were linked from the official NTA JEE Main homepage at the time this catalogue was prepared (12 November 2026). They cover the NTA homepage's listed April 2026 B.Tech papers: 2 Apr (Shifts 1–2), 4 Apr (Shifts 1–2), 5 Apr (Shifts 1–2), 6 Apr (Shifts 1–2), and 8 Apr (Shift 2).

NTA's official documents archive also lists 2026 Session-I and Session-II Paper 1 material and final answer-key documents. The 2026 homepage's direct paper menu is not assumed to be an exhaustive index of every response-sheet paper.

## 2022–2025
The current app links to the official NTA Documents archive as the year-specific entry point. Individual dates/shifts have not yet been independently catalogued in this release. Do not infer a number of papers from these archive links.

## Import rules
1. Preserve original source URL and paper metadata.
2. Match answers using NTA final answer keys, not provisional keys, when available.
3. Keep NTA question IDs and option IDs for traceability.
4. Keep question text, options, diagrams and worked solutions separate from metadata.
5. Do not import third-party proprietary question/solution databases without permission.
6. Tag subject/chapter/topic only after review; record the classifier and reviewer.
7. Mark missing, dropped or ambiguous answer-key items explicitly.
